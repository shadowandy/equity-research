# Funds (ETFs, unit trusts, listed funds)

A fund has no management guidance, earnings, or directors dealing in its units; its value is its holdings. Fill the same template, with these changes.

- **Sources**: the manager's factsheet and fund page (NAV, fund size, units outstanding, TER, tracking, top holdings), distribution announcements (amounts, ex-dates, return-of-capital share), the annual and semi-annual reports, and the exchange's announcements for unitholder notices.
- **Recent Financial Metrics** rows: NAV per unit, fund size, TER, tracking difference against the benchmark (1y and 3y), trailing distributions and yield, return-of-capital share of the latest distribution, top-3 concentration.
- **Forward Outlook**: broker coverage of the fund, then guidance from the largest holdings, each with its weight.
- **Peer table**: funds with the same mandate, wherever listed. Columns `AUM (USD) | TER | Yield (ttm) | P/B (look-through) | 1y NAV return`.
- **Valuation**: scenarios move the look-through multiple and the NAV it applies to; the price follows NAV.
- **Bold labels**: `Company risks` reads **Fund risks** (concentration, tracking, currency of holdings, return of capital, holdings' equity raises). `Insider dealings` reads **Unitholder notices** (substantial-unitholder filings). `Buybacks` reads **Creations & redemptions** (units outstanding and net flows; a top holding's buyback where material). `Institutional ownership` keeps its label.
