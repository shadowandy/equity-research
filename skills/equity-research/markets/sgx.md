# SGX (Singapore Exchange)

- **Counters**: most trade in SGD; some have dual SGD/USD counters (different ticker codes, same share). Report the counter the user named. Board lot is 100 shares.
- **Reporting cadence**: half-yearly results are mandatory; many companies dropped quarterly reporting after 2020, so "QoQ" is often unavailable. Use HoH and YoY, and business updates where issued.
- **Filings and announcements**: SGX company announcements (sgx.com/securities/company-announcements), the company's investor relations page for results decks and annual reports.
- **Insider activity**: directors' and substantial shareholders' interest notifications on SGX announcements (filed within 2 business days). Amounts are often share counts only; compute value from the transaction price stated in the notice.
- **Buybacks**: daily share buy-back notices on SGX announcements; cumulative count against the mandate limit.
- **Options**: no meaningful listed single-stock options market. Write "Not available" for options flow. Short-sell volume (SGX publishes daily short sell reports) is an acceptable labelled proxy for positioning.
- **Analyst coverage**: local brokers (DBS Group Research, OCBC Investment Research, UOB Kay Hian, Maybank Securities, CGS International, Phillip Securities) plus global banks for STI names. The Edge Singapore and The Business Times report target changes.
- **Benchmarks**: Straits Times Index (STI); SPDR STI ETF (ES3) as the market ETF. iEdge sector indices or regional sector peers for sector comparison.
- **REITs and trusts**: value on DPU yield, P/NAV, aggregate leverage (MAS limit 50%), interest coverage ratio, WALE, occupancy.
