# LSE (London Stock Exchange)

- **Currency**: shares are quoted in pence (GBX). Prices and targets in pence (1,245p); financials in the reporting currency, which for many large caps is USD (Shell, HSBC, BP, Rio Tinto, AstraZeneca). Header reads "GBX Counter".
- **Segments**: Main Market vs AIM. AIM companies follow lighter disclosure rules; say which segment applies.
- **Reporting cadence**: half-year and full-year results; many large caps add quarterly trading updates.
- **Filings and announcements**: RNS (londonstockexchange.com news), company investor relations, Companies House for filings history.
- **Insider activity**: PDMR notifications under UK MAR Article 19 (via RNS, within 3 business days). Distinguish open-market trades from share-plan vesting and dividend reinvestment.
- **Major holdings**: TR-1 notifications when holdings cross 3% and each 1% after.
- **Buybacks**: daily "Transaction in own shares" RNS announcements.
- **Short interest**: FCA publishes net short positions at or above 0.5% of issued share capital.
- **Options**: ICE Futures Europe lists single-stock options but liquidity is thin outside large caps. For names with US ADRs, ADR options data is the usual labelled proxy.
- **Benchmarks**: FTSE 100, FTSE 250, FTSE All-Share; iShares Core FTSE 100 (ISF) as the market ETF; FTSE sector indices for sector comparison.
