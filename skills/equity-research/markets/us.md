# US (NYSE, NASDAQ)

- **Reporting cadence**: quarterly 10-Q, annual 10-K, material events in 8-K; earnings call transcripts usually available. Foreign private issuers file 20-F/6-K instead and may report half-yearly.
- **Filings**: SEC EDGAR (sec.gov/edgar), company investor relations.
- **Insider activity**: Form 4 (filed within 2 business days) with exact share counts, prices, and values. Flag Rule 10b5-1 planned sales separately from discretionary trades; cluster buys by several insiders are the stronger signal.
- **Institutional ownership**: 13F filings (quarterly, up to 45 days after quarter end, so state the as-of quarter); 13D/13G for 5%+ holders.
- **Buybacks**: authorisation in 8-K or earnings releases; executed amounts in the 10-Q/10-K.
- **Short interest**: FINRA short interest (published twice monthly) with days-to-cover.
- **Options**: liquid listed options for most names. Put/call ratio, IV and IV rank, unusual activity from options data sites (e.g. Barchart, Market Chameleon); state the date of the snapshot.
- **Benchmarks**: S&P 500 (SPY) or Nasdaq-100 (QQQ) for the market; Select Sector SPDR ETFs (XLK, XLF, XLE, ...) or industry ETFs for sector comparison.
