# HKEX (Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing)

- **Counters**: HKD counter is primary; some names have an RMB counter under the dual-counter model (separate ticker code, e.g. 8xxxx). Board lots vary by stock; state it when relevant to sizing.
- **Share classes**: H-shares and red chips often have an A-share listing too; note the A/H premium where one exists.
- **Reporting currency**: frequently RMB (or USD); prices stay in HKD with the FX rate stated in valuations.
- **Reporting cadence**: interim and annual results mandatory; many tech and larger names also report quarterly.
- **Filings and announcements**: HKEXnews (hkexnews.hk) for announcements, results, circulars.
- **Insider activity**: Disclosure of Interests (di.hkex.com.hk) for directors and substantial shareholders (5%+). Notices include share counts and average price; compute values.
- **Ownership shifts**: CCASS shareholding search for custodian-level changes, and Southbound Stock Connect holdings (HKEXnews) for mainland investor flows.
- **Buybacks**: Next Day Disclosure Returns on HKEXnews.
- **Short interest**: HKEX daily short selling turnover; SFC aggregated reportable short positions (weekly).
- **Options**: HKEX lists stock options on major names with published daily statistics. For dual-listed US names (e.g. Alibaba, JD, Baidu), US-listed options are a richer labelled proxy.
- **Benchmarks**: Hang Seng Index, Hang Seng China Enterprises Index, Hang Seng TECH; Tracker Fund of Hong Kong (2800) as the market ETF.
