# Other exchanges

Before researching, establish for this market:

- **Announcement platform**: the exchange's official company announcements site and the securities regulator's filing system.
- **Reporting cadence**: quarterly vs half-yearly; set "QoQ" or "HoH" accordingly.
- **Insider regime**: which disclosure covers director dealings and major holdings, its threshold, and filing deadline.
- **Buyback disclosure**: where executed repurchases are published.
- **Short interest and options**: whether either is published or listed. If the company has an ADR or secondary listing in the US, London, or Hong Kong, that listing's data is the usual labelled proxy.
- **Benchmarks**: the main market index and a tradable ETF tracking it; a sector index or regional sector ETF.
- **Currency**: counter currency and reporting currency; quote convention (some markets quote in minor units, like pence or agorot).

Record each answer as a fact-sheet line with its source, then proceed with the main research passes.
